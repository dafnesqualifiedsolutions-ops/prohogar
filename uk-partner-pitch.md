## Partner Agreement Summary
- 50% of setup fee, paid within 7 days of client payment
- 40% of monthly payments, paid monthly while client is active
- 6-month tail on clients you sourced if you stop working with us
- Source of truth: leads attributed to partner who first introduced them
- Partners may not offer competing AI receptionist services during agreement + 12 months after
- Fair usage limits: 300 mins/month included per client; overages at £0.50/min
- If client's Vapi costs exceed 60% of monthly payment for 2 consecutive months, we may adjust pricing
